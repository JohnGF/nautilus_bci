Purpose:
Simple eye tracker paradigm.

Description:
Shows a white fixation cross on black background in the middle of the screen for a randomized duration between 2.75 and 3.25 seconds.
Afterward the fixation cross disappears a white ball is shown for 3 seconds in one of the corners of the window.

Approx. Duration:
3:20

Groups:
1 ... Top Left Corner
2 ... Top Right Corner
3 ... Bottom Left Corner
4 ... Bottom Right Corner
 
Instructions to the patient:
Your task is to focus the fixation cross and during the task period the white ball.
